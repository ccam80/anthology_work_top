\section{Cubie: GPU numerical integration through Python}
Cubie runs numerical integration kernels on domestic CUDA cards. It integrates
one system of equations per thread, a workload that is arithmetically intense
and keeps a wide range of values live. The problem is traditionally treated as
non-parallelisable, but modern GPUs can carry the per-thread load. In my draft
comparisons (pending publication), Cubie is about 10,000$\times$ faster than
the engineering-standard integrators in MATLAB and SciPy, and about
100$\times$ faster than its nearest competitor, DiffEqGPU.jl from the SciML
team at MIT. Cubie JIT-compiles its kernels through numba-cuda-mlir, so
compile-time choices such as memory placement and loop unrolling adapt to
each user's problem.
